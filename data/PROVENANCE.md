# Where the datasets come from

Each `data/<name>/solvated.pdb.gz` is one solvated system, loaded by
[`datasets.py`](datasets.py). They are the systems the OpenMM plugin's tests and
benchmarks run on.

The `water_10k/solvated.pdb.gz` snapshot, NPT-equilibrated at 300 K and 1 bar,
also serves the LTO-IR Coulomb/C6 example. It follows the same filename convention and is included in automatic
benchmark dataset discovery; see its README for preparation and reference generation.

| Datasets | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bace`, `cdk2`, `jnk1`, `mcl1`, `p38`, `ptp1b`, `thrombin`, `tyk2` | The proteins of the JACS benchmark set as distributed by [OpenFreeEnergy/openfe-benchmarks](https://github.com/OpenFreeEnergy/openfe-benchmarks) (`openfe_benchmarks/data/benchmark_systems/jacs_set/<name>/protein.pdb`, atom for atom), with missing atoms and hydrogens added (PDBFixer, pH 7), solvated in TIP3P-FB water with 0.15 M NaCl and 1 nm padding, minimised and equilibrated (200 ps restrained, then 5 ns unrestrained NPT) with Amber14 and OpenMM 8.4 | MIT (openfe-benchmarks), below |
| `bace_dodeca`, `bace_octa`, `cdk2_dodeca`, `cdk2_octa`, `p38_dodeca`, `p38_octa`, `tyk2_dodeca`, `tyk2_octa` | The solutes of `bace`, `cdk2`, `p38` and `tyk2` re-solvated by NVIDIA in a rhombic dodecahedron and a truncated octahedron of the original box's width, with the original's ion concentrations, minimised and equilibrated with OpenMM | MIT (openfe-benchmarks), below, for the solutes |
| `bace_tip4p`, `cdk2_tip4p`, `tyk2_tip4p` | The solutes of `bace`, `cdk2` and `tyk2` re-solvated by NVIDIA in TIP4P-Ew water | MIT (openfe-benchmarks), below, for the solutes |
| `water_10k` | Generated by NVIDIA with OpenMM 8.4; 3,375 waters (10,125 atoms). Originally created 2026-04-06, then made whole across periodic boundaries, energy-minimized as preparation, and NPT-equilibrated at 300 K and 1 bar. The accompanying reference is independently calculated with OpenMM and JAX from the final NPT snapshot and box; see [`water_10k/README.md`](water_10k/README.md) | Apache-2.0 |
| `water_100k` | A box of 35,546 TIP3P-FB waters built and equilibrated by NVIDIA with OpenMM 8.4 | Apache-2.0, as this repository |
| `tyk2_alchemical/` | TYK2 alchemical fixtures; see [`tyk2_alchemical/PROVENANCE.md`](tyk2_alchemical/PROVENANCE.md) | MIT (openfe-benchmarks), below, for the inputs |

## apoa1 and dhfr

The tests and benchmarks also run on apoa1 and dhfr, which are not in `data/`
and are not distributed with this repository. They are OpenMM's benchmark
systems `examples/benchmarks/apoa1.pdb` and
`examples/benchmarks/5dfr_solv-cube_equil.pdb` of
[openmm/openmm](https://github.com/openmm/openmm). At test time
the OpenMM plugin's
[`_benchmark_systems.py`](../plugins/openmm/python/nv_openmm_plugin/_benchmark_systems.py) reads them from the user's OpenMM
installation (conda-forge's `openmm` package installs them under
`share/openmm/examples/benchmarks/`), or, when that has none and `CI` is not set,
downloads them from OpenMM's repository at release 8.4.0, checked against the SHA-256 it
records.
